## Could not confirm

- **Ads on the free tier.** JEFIT's Sept 2026 comparison says the free tier has no ads, but the Elite page still sells "ad-free" and three third-party reviews describe the free tier as ad supported. Needs a hands-on check in Phase 4.
- **CAD pricing.** Taken from one fetch of the Canadian App Store ($16.49/month, $88.99/year, plus several legacy and promo SKUs). Fetch output listed some SKUs ambiguously. Verify in the app on a Canadian account.
- **Free trial.** No trial length found on official pages.
- **Watch support tiering.** The Elite page lists watch support as Elite; one store mirror says only "unlimited" smartwatch workouts are Elite. Unclear what free users get.
- **Whether the friend list and Compare Records still exist in the current app.** The Records post is from 2021; the Sept 2026 JEFIT post confirms friends and following, but not the compare view.
- **Coach Mode pricing and whether two non-trainers could use it as a partner link.** Not stated.
- **Review dates.** JustUseApp did not show dates per review, so the sample may include reviews older than 2025.
- **Reddit (r/jefit) and Trustpilot.** Reddit was blocked and the redlib mirror disallowed by robots.txt; Trustpilot returned 403. Community scan is thin and should be spot-checked by Josh.
- **Google Play rating and review count.** The Play fetch returned downloads (5M+) but not the rating.
- **User count.** JEFIT claims 12M+ (App Store) and 13M+ (blog). These are company claims, not third-party estimates. No revenue or download estimates beyond the Play Store 5M+ band were used.
