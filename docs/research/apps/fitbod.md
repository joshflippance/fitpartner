## Takeaways for FitPartner

- **Borrow:**
  - Per-person tailoring of a shared workout: Fitbod's share link keeps the same exercises but scales sets, reps and weight to each person. FitPartner can let one partner plan a session and auto-scale it for the other.
  - Muscle recovery heat map and a single Strength Score as a quick "how am I doing" glance; both translate well to a side-by-side card.
  - Shareable post-workout summary image as a lightweight growth loop.
- **Beat:**
  - Fitbod's Duo Plan is sold "for couples" at USD 159.99/year but gives zero shared visibility. FitPartner's core promise (real-time shared dashboard plus shared weekly streak) is exactly the gap. Use this contrast in positioning.
  - Price anchor: a couple pays USD 159.99/year on Fitbod Duo for lifting only, with no nutrition. A FitPartner couples price under that, covering calories, protein, weight and lifting, has a clear value story.
  - No nutrition or protein tracking in Fitbod, so couples need a second app. One app for both is a beat.
  - Billing trust: trial auto-renew and refund friction is the top complaint theme. Clear trial terms and easy cancellation are cheap wins.
- **Skip:**
  - Building a proprietary progression algorithm. It is Fitbod's moat (150M+ workouts) and still draws complaints about repetition and calibration. Simple progression rules are enough for FitPartner.
  - Large video exercise library. Expensive to produce and not the couples differentiator.
  - Watch apps for now. Fitbod's watch apps generate a steady stream of sync complaints.
