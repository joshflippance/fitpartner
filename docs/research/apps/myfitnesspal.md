## Review themes

Sources for shares: Unstar analysis of 300 recent Google Play reviews plus 49 recent App Store reviews, skewed to 1 to 3 stars [18]; MyNetDiary scorecard of 752 MyFitnessPal reviews from March 2026 [19]; Trustpilot (4.3 from about 13,179 reviews) [20]; App Store Canada (4.6 from about 236K ratings) [7]; r/Myfitnesspal recent posts [21]. Shares below are approximate shares of recent written complaint reviews, not of all users.

|---|---|---|---|
| Paywall creep, especially the barcode scanner moving to Premium | Complaint | About 30 to 40% (29% general paywall plus 13% naming barcode, overlapping) | A long time user feels the free app was gutted so basic scanning now costs money. |
| April 2026 redesign (Today tab replaced the Diary home) | Complaint | About 11% overall, much higher in April to May 2026 | Logging now takes more taps and the per meal macro view is hard to find, so a subscriber cancelled. |
| Bugs: sync failures, logouts, lost history, broken weight graphs | Complaint | About 15 to 20% | Watch shows as connected but steps never arrive, and the weight chart freezes. |

Partner / couple mentions: 0 explicit partner, spouse or couple mentions found in the samples read (App Store Canada visible reviews, Trustpilot summary, Kimola Google Play preview of 30 reviews, Unstar complaint clusters, r/Myfitnesspal search for friends and husband, wife or partner). The community forum Friends tag is full of people asking strangers to be accountability friends, which shows demand for social support but not for a couple feature [16]. Couple oriented roundups (written by competitor Nutrola, so biased) say couples want shared recipes with different portions per person, one consistent entry for the same meal, and separate calorie and macro goals, and that MyFitnessPal's link based recipe sharing and crowdsourced entries make that clumsy [22] (unverified), [23] (unverified).

Third-party estimates (not official): about 900K monthly downloads and about US$13M monthly revenue as of March 2026, Sensor Tower data as reported by Fitt Insider (corrected in cross-check) [12]. Wikipedia cites 280M users in 2026 [24] (unverified).

## Takeaways for FitPartner

- **Borrow:** Diary privacy levels (public, friends only, private) as a simple model for what a partner can see. Copy a meal from a friend's diary, adapted as "log what my partner logged" with my own portion. Small streak celebrations at first log and early milestones, which MyFitnessPal added in 2025. Weight trend graph as a core screen, since broken graphs drew heavy anger.
- **Beat:** Social in MyFitnessPal is a passive friends list with no shared view, no shared goals and no challenges, and the newsfeed appears gone. FitPartner's real time two person dashboard and shared weekly streak fill a gap the market leader does not cover. Keep barcode scan and basic logging free or cheap, since paywalling them is the single biggest complaint. Keep logging to the fewest taps; the 2026 redesign shows users punish added taps fast. Use one shared food entry per household meal so both partners see the same numbers.
- **Skip:** Competing on database size or AI photo logging depth, where MyFitnessPal plus Cal AI will out spend us. Meal planner, recipes catalogue and grocery delivery. A public forum or community; it is moderation heavy and users there want accountability, which a partner already provides. Full screen ads.
