**Other notable features:** Apple Watch app with standalone logging, Live Activity and Dynamic Island rest timer, home screen calendar and activity widgets, PR and 1RM records, supersets, RPE, warm up and drop set tags, Siri Shortcuts, CSV export. Nutrition is import only: calories and macros can be pulled from Apple Health or Google Fit (from apps like MyFitnessPal or Lose It) and shown on profile widgets, or calories can be typed in manually as a measurement. [1], [2], [6], [8]

**Release cadence:** Version 6.5.0 (Live Activity rest timer, Apple Health import fixes) is the latest iOS release, dated Aug 12. Google Play last update is listed as 2026-03-11 by a third-party tracker (unverified). Either way, releases are slow compared with Hevy. [2], [10], [9], [12]

**Ratings:** App Store Canada about 4.8 from 13K+ ratings [2]. **Web access:** The official homepage says Strong Cloud lets you reach your training on any device, but no web logging app was found. Treat web as absent until Phase 4 confirms. [1]

## Takeaways for FitPartner

- **Borrow:** Strong's speed bar for set logging: previous weights pre-filled, one tap to complete a set, auto rest timer. Copy the "your data is never locked" promise for the free tier, which earns goodwill. Live Activity or lock screen rest timer and a simple calendar widget are cheap wins.
- **Borrow:** Template sharing by link is a useful seed: let one partner build a routine and the other adopt it in one tap, but keep it live and linked rather than a one-time copy.
- **Beat:** Strong has zero shared progress. A couple who both use Strong must screenshot or text each other. FitPartner's shared dashboard and joint weekly streak fill a gap that Strong has not touched in years.
- **Beat:** Strong charges per person with no family plan (CAD 39.99 per year each, so about CAD 80 for a couple). A single couples price below two Strong subscriptions is a clear pitch.
- **Beat:** Strong only imports nutrition. FitPartner puts calories, protein, weight and lifting in one view, so couples do not need Strong plus a separate food app.
- **Skip:** Do not paywall charts or cap routines at a tiny number. It is Strong's top complaint. Do not chase a deep AI coach to compete here; Strong users actively value no AI, and the wedge is the partner layer, not programming.
- **Skip:** A public social feed. Strong's audience values privacy, and FitPartner's two-person scope is the right size.
