## Review themes

Sample: about 20 recent and "most helpful" App Store reviews (US and Canada storefronts), Trustpilot (96 reviews, TrustScore 1.7), one third-party analysis of 8,934 App Store and Google Play reviews from Jan 2024 to Mar 2026 (published by a competitor), third-party roundups, and a Reddit mention aggregator (33 mentions). Direct Reddit and Google Play pages could not be loaded. Complaint shares below come from the 8,934 review analysis [16] and are a single competitor source (unverified). Praise shares are rough estimates from the store sample and are not measured.

| Theme | Praise or complaint | Approx. share | Example (paraphrased) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simple, approachable logging that works for long term weight loss | Praise | Most 4 and 5 star reviews in the store sample; Reddit aggregator rates it about 4.2 of 5 | A long time user lost 40 lb and has kept it off for nine years by logging daily. [1][17] |
| Subscription cost and paywall creep (features moving from free to Premium, barcode scanner, macros) | Complaint | About 25% cite cost, about 15% cite feature removal or paywalling (unverified) | A former fan says the app used to beat rivals but is now so paywalled they switched back. [16][3] |
| Ads on the free tier, including video ads after logging | Complaint | About 19% (unverified) | A reviewer says video ads now play after each meal is logged, which ruins the flow. [16][3] |
| Food database accuracy (user entries, duplicates, wrong serving sizes) | Complaint | About 17% (unverified) | A happy user still double checks barcode results because portions are sometimes wrong. [16][1] |
| Snap It photo logging accuracy | Mixed, leaning complaint | Not measured; recurring in roundups and some store reviews | A long time user asked for the photo feature to be removed, saying its guesses are too far off for beginners. [1][12][2] |
| Billing, renewals, and support responsiveness | Complaint | Dominant on Trustpilot (small, self selected sample) | Reviewers describe being charged for both yearly and lifetime, and slow or bot only support. [18][3] |
| Redesign and UI changes | Complaint | About 9% cite UI complexity or design (unverified) | A Trustpilot reviewer says the new version feels like an unfinished first release. [16][18] |
| Community safety (groups and social areas) | Complaint | Rare, but notable for social design | One reviewer worried that community groups attracted minors discussing disordered eating; another reported scammers in the community. [3][18] |

Partner / couple mentions: very low. In roughly 20 store reviews read, zero mentioned a partner or spouse. The Reddit aggregator found no partner or friend usage among 33 mentions [17]. The clearest signal is a 2021 hands-on review in which the writer could not add their spouse as a friend and had to screenshot recipes to share them [14]. A competitor's couples roundup says couples can run weight loss challenges together in Lose It!, but describes no shared view [15] (unverified). What people ask for, where it comes up: adding a spouse easily, sharing recipes and meals between accounts, and one subscription covering two people (official help center confirms this is not offered) [14][5].

## Takeaways for FitPartner

- **Borrow:**
  - The single big number: a daily calorie budget with "I'm done logging" closing the day, then a projection of goal date based on the current logging streak. Simple and motivating. [10]
  - Voice logging as a fast path ("2 eggs and toast" in one sentence). It is the quickest way to beat database search for a two person household.
  - Separate ad removal and data toggles (they let users hide GLP-1 content). Respectful controls build trust.
- **Beat:**
  - Two people, one plan: Lose It! is strictly one user per subscription with no family sharing. A single FitPartner price for a pair directly answers what couples ask for. [5]
  - Real shared view: Lose It! friends are an email invite with undocumented visibility and no side by side dashboard. FitPartner's real time two person dashboard and shared weekly streak have no equivalent here.
  - Shared meals: let one partner log a meal for both (portion split), which Lose It! cannot do and reviewers have asked for. [14]
  - Lifting: Lose It! only logs exercise as calorie burn. Sets, reps and progression are an open lane.
  - Free tier trust: Lose It! is pulling barcode scanning behind the paywall and roughly doubled its list price in 2026; users notice. Keep core logging free and ad free. [6][4]
- **Skip:**
  - Open community groups and public challenges with strangers: moderation burden and safety complaints. Keep social private to the pair. [3][18]
  - Affiliate and GLP-1 partner promotion cards in the main UI.
  - Lifetime upsells and stacked subscriptions that produce double billing complaints. [18][5]
